§ 04 · RUN

One call.
One signed dossier.

The API is small on purpose. Send a PDF; receive the same finding graph you inspected on the rail. Every field maps back to a rail address.

$ TERMINAL · SAMPLE req_8fk2 
$ curl https://api.colophon.dev/v1/analyze \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer $CO_KEY" \
    -F "document=@hdfc_apr25.pdf"

↳ 4.8s · signed dossier returned inline · SAMPLE
✗ FAIL · req_8fk2 · 4 findings
   × BALANCE_BREAK        p4·r17
   × PRODUCER_MISMATCH    p4·r03
   ~ FONT_METRIC_SHIFT    p4·r08
   ~ OCR_LOW_CONFIDENCE   p4·r14

dossier → req_8fk2.dossier.pdf (signed)
verify  → colophon verify req_8fk2
STEP 01 · SEND
One multipart POST.
Files up to 50 pages, 20 MB. Timeout 30 s. Retries idempotent by content hash.
STEP 02 · RECEIVE
The full evidence graph.
Every finding carries page, box, expected/found, marker id — every value points back to the rail address you can screenshot.
STEP 03 · VERIFY
Ed25519, offline.
Anyone can verify a dossier against the published key — your auditor, a regulator, a customer — with no account.
